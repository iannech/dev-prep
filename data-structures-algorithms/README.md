## 🎯 Three Study Modes

### Mode 1: Light Days (1 hour)
- Solve 1-2 easy-medium problems
- Focus on understanding, not speed
- Good for busy work days

### Mode 2: Deep Days (2 hours)
- Solve 2-3 medium-hard problems
- Include optimization and review
- Best for weekends

### Mode 3: Review Days (45 min)
- Re-solve problems from past weeks
- Speed runs (solve quickly without hints)
- Spaced repetition maintenance

---

## 🚨 Critical Success Factors

1. **Consistency over Intensity**
   - 5-6 days/week is better than 1 day/week for 8 hours
   - Miss a day? Just pick up the next day, don't binge

2. **Understanding over Speed**
   - Weeks 1-3: Focus on understanding patterns
   - Weeks 4-6: Develop pattern recognition
   - Weeks 7-8: Build speed with repetition

3. **Communication Practice**
   - Always talk through approach before coding
   - Explain your logic while coding
   - This alone can improve interview performance by 30%

4. **Mock Interviews**
   - Don't skip Week 7-8 mock interviews
   - Real pressure simulation = irreplaceable practice
   - Invest in 2-3 quality mocks (Interviewing.io recommended)

5. **Weak Area Focus**
   - Track which topics trip you up
   - Spend 20% extra time on weak areas
   - Re-solve 5+ problems in weak category in Week 7
